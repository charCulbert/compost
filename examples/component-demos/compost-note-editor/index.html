Editor 1/16 · snapping · 2 bars

Drag a note to move it, its edges to trim either end, Command-drag for velocity, Alt-drag to copy, drag on empty grid to box-select, drag the ruler to select time, Shift-click to extend, double-click to add; draw mode drags right for length and vertically for velocity. The start/end and loop markers drag independently; the loop bar also moves from its middle. Delete, arrows (Shift-up/down for octaves, Shift-left/right fine, Alt-left/right length; Fold up/down steps through visible pitches), Cmd-A, Cmd-D duplicates one span later, Cmd-L loops to the selection, Q quantizes starts, Shift-Q includes lengths. Wheel scrolls pitch, Shift-wheel time, Cmd-wheel zooms time, Alt-wheel zooms pitch; drag the keybed vertically to scroll or horizontally to zoom pitch. The playhead is a position the host supplies.
