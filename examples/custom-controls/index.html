What this demonstrates

This page owns the canvas drawing and hit testing. Compost turns its waveform and pad grid into two independent numeric controls, supplying parameter events, host-value synchronization, dragging, keyboard operation, and accessible slider semantics.

How to use: Drag the waveform or pad grid up to increase and down to decrease. Tab moves between their semantic controls; Shift makes dragging finer, and Escape cancels an active drag.
