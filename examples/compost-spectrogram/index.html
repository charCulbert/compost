Demo consumer: loads and plays the audio, turns it into FFT values, optionally combines those values into 96 Mel bands, and sends each result with its time and centre frequencies. The analyser starts when playback begins.

Compost component: never reads or analyses audio. It copies the supplied numbers, keeps a bounded history, places them by time and frequency, maps their values to colours, scrolls the visible time window, and draws the canvas.
